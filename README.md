# Producer

Project interpretation, direct delegation, delivery and learning.

This is an independent, project-neutral skill. Its entry point is [SKILL.md](skills/producer/SKILL.md). Install the complete `skills/producer` directory, including its references. It can be used directly; no ECD studio, producer or other department is a prerequisite.

Project objectives, audiences, styles, formats, research conclusions, operational targets and permissions belong to the project and are supplied per task. They must not become defaults in this skill or its supporting files.

Version 1.1.0-alpha adds requirements-to-evidence links, decision rationale and replacement history, actionable dependencies, evidence of handoff acceptance, and follow-through on method improvements. The methods apply according to the work; small edits can proceed directly. Sources and transfer limits are documented in [method-basis.md](skills/producer/references/method-basis.md).

This remains an alpha. Independent agents produced and checked local artifacts from synthetic requests, including matched before/after continuity, project-context and small-edit tasks. Additional trials covered decision changes, incomplete repairs and handoff continuation. The prior version also succeeded on the matched tasks, so these checks support preserved behavior and bounded method use, not a measured gain in reliability. They do not establish long-term organizational effects or real external scheduling performance. Full research and test materials remain in the maintenance project, outside the installable skill and distribution repository.
